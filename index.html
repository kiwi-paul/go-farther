Who we are

Five fathers, five musical roads

Hailing from the Gold Coast hinterland, Go Farther is a five piece band of fathers, each coming from a different musical road. The result is a melting pot concocted of bastardised rock songs that happen because they happen, not because anyone planned them.

Our sound is driven by gritty guitars, solid rhythms, and a pinch of brass. Always moving forward, always carrying momentum, always going further than you planned.

Go Farther is about the long drive, the detour, the refusal to slow down. Wherever the road takes us, we are heading there together, and whatever comes out along the way is exactly what it is meant to be.

Two Battle of the Bands finals inside our first eight gigs. Second at Den Devine, third at Passport to Airlie.

  • Vocals Shayne Natoli
  • Guitar, trumpet Chris Hari
  • Guitar Warren Tucker
  • Drums Richie Pattison
  • Bass Paul Holland
